## Transcript

For the capsulorhexis, I use a needle cystotome. I start with the area away from the main port, fold the flap, and the fold of this flap will guide me to have the perfectly round capsulorhexis. Every couple of clock hours, I will regrasp the flap and continue until we get a perfect central circle of approximately five point five millimeter.

I then proceed to do my main port incision, which I personally make after the rhexis so that the anterior chamber remains full and I do not lose any viscoelastic during my capsulorhexis.
