[Phacoemulsification for beginners]
In hydrodissection I use a Chang cannula. We want to make sure that the tip is between the intracapsular leaflet and the cortex and not between nucleus and cortex. We want to make sure that it's a real hydrodissection between the capsule and the lens. Hydrodissection is about high pressure and low volume. We want pulses of high pressure. High volume is what burps the lens into the anterior chamber and prolapses the iris through the wound.

[Stop and chop]
Hydrodissection is done by decompressing the anterior chamber slightly and then injecting balanced salt solution under the anterior capsule. I do this in at least two areas. Look for the wave, then decompress the nucleus to release the fluid from below the nucleus before injecting any more fluid. You can also do a hydrodelineation into the matter of the nucleus; you can see that golden ring sign, which is a classic sign of a successful delineation, separating endonucleus and epinucleus. Rotate the nucleus well to break all adhesions.

[Divide and conquer]
I will do minimal hydro enough for me to see the hydro wave crossing across and then just rock the nucleus.
