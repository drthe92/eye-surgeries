[Phacoemulsification for beginners]
When we're injecting Provisc, or cohesive viscoelastic, we want to fill the bag first. You can't hit the posterior capsule with the cannula as long as there is viscoelastic actively being injected into the eye. It creates a fluid wave that protects the capsule. We fill the bag first and then the anterior chamber. If we fill the anterior chamber first, we risk pushing the posterior capsule leaflet back and not getting a full capsular bag with space for the lens. I go in bevel up, that gives me a little more control of the eye without having to use a second instrument to stabilize. Make sure the leading haptic is in the capsular bag. Push this lens straight down with the Connor; get the haptic going out towards the equator, make sure it's in the capsular bag. I usually remove the viscoelastic in the anterior chamber first, then slide the lens over by pushing down on it, and once I can get around the edge, lift up.

[Stop and chop]
IOL implantation is done with a 2.4 cartridge. You must stabilize the eye and watch the cornea to avoid depressing the cornea or pressing on the cornea with the cartridge. The entry of the cartridge tip should be a gentle sliding motion and inject the lens directly into the capsular bag, which should be adequately inflated first with dispersive viscoelastic. Always ensure that the leading haptic is in the capsular bag before advancing the piston. If the trailing haptic opens outside the bag, it will have to be flopped into the bag gently or dialed depending on the IOL. Viscoelastic removal is very important: remnant viscoelastic can cause increased IOP, fibrosis, and pain. Removal mode with high vacuum should be used; create a little turbulence to remove viscoelastic stuck in the angles and on the cornea. Then go below the lens using only irrigation, push the lens slightly away, go below the IOL in the bag, lift the lens up to remove remaining viscoelastic trapped in the bag. Seal the wounds using hydration: hydrate the side ports first in one direction, then the main port, especially the roof. The eye should be watertight but not too rigid.
